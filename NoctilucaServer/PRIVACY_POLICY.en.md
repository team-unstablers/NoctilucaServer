Noctiluca Privacy Policy
========================

Last Updated: 2026-03-04
Provider: team unstablers Inc. ("Company")
Product: Noctiluca Server and related components ("Software")

This English translation is provided for reference purposes only. In the event
of any discrepancy between the Korean original (PRIVACY_POLICY.md) and this
English translation, the Korean original shall prevail.

The Company establishes and discloses this Privacy Policy in accordance with the
Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea to protect the
personal information of data subjects and to promptly and smoothly handle
related grievances.


1. Purpose of Processing Personal Information

The Company processes personal information for the following purposes. Personal
information collected shall not be used for purposes other than the following,
and if the purpose of use changes, necessary measures such as obtaining separate
consent will be taken.

  (a) License Verification and Prevention of Unauthorized Copying
      The Company processes device identifiers to verify the validity of
      software licenses and manage seat limits.

  (b) Software Quality Improvement (Telemetry)
      The Company processes error reporting data solely when users have
      explicitly opted in, for the purpose of diagnosing software errors
      and improving quality.


2. Categories of Personal Information Processed

2.1 License Authentication Data

  - Items collected: Device Identifier
  - Time of collection: Upon license activation
  - Method of collection: Automatically transmitted by the Software

2.2 Telemetry and Error Reporting Data

  - Items collected: System version (OS), error messages, stack traces,
    and comparable program execution context information
  - Time of collection: When errors occur during Software execution,
    only if the user has explicitly enabled (opted in to) telemetry
  - Method of collection: Automatically transmitted by the Software
  - Regional restriction: Telemetry cannot be enabled on devices whose
    macOS region setting corresponds to a country within the European
    Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom. No telemetry data is
    collected from such devices.
  - Privacy safeguards:
    - Personally identifiable information (PII) is masked before transmission.
    - Data is transmitted on a session basis with a randomly assigned
      identifier, which users may reset at any time.


3. Retention Period of Personal Information

The Company processes and retains personal information within the period agreed
upon at the time of collection or as required by applicable laws.

  (a) Device Identifier: Retained until the license is revoked on the
      applicable device.
  (b) Telemetry Data: Retained for a maximum of 6 months from the date of
      collection, then deleted.


4. Provision of Personal Information to Third Parties

The Company processes personal information only within the scope specified in
Section 1 and provides personal information to third parties only in cases
falling under Articles 17 and 18 of the Personal Information Protection Act,
such as with the consent of the data subject or under special provisions of law.

Currently, the Company does not provide personal information to third parties.


5. Entrustment of Personal Information Processing and Overseas Transfer

The Company entrusts the processing of personal information as follows for
smooth service provision.

  - Entrusted party: Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry)
  - Entrusted tasks: Collection, storage, and processing of telemetry and
    error reporting data
  - Items transferred: Telemetry data as described in Section 2.2
  - Country of transfer: United States
  - Timing and method of transfer: Transmitted via network when telemetry
    data is generated
  - Retention period: Up to 6 months from the date of collection

Any changes to the entrusted tasks or the entrusted party will be disclosed
through this Privacy Policy.


6. Procedures and Methods for Destruction of Personal Information

The Company destroys personal information without delay when it is no longer
needed, such as upon expiration of the retention period or achievement of the
processing purpose.

  (a) Device Identifier: Deleted from Company servers upon license revocation.
  (b) Telemetry Data: Automatically deleted from the Sentry platform upon
      expiration of the retention period (6 months).

Electronic file-format information is destroyed using technical methods that
prevent recovery or reproduction.


7. Rights of Data Subjects and Methods of Exercise

Data subjects may exercise the following rights regarding their personal
information at any time:

  (a) Request to suspend processing of personal information
  (b) Request to delete personal information
  (c) Request to access personal information

Data subjects may directly exercise their rights through the following methods:

  - Disable (opt out of) the telemetry feature in the Software settings to
    stop data collection.
  - Reset the session identifier at any time in the Software settings.
  - Revoke the license on a specific device through the management page or
    procedures provided by the Company to request deletion of the device
    identifier.

Rights may also be exercised in writing or by email, and the Company will take
action without delay.


8. Measures to Ensure the Security of Personal Information

The Company takes the following measures to ensure the security of personal
information:

  (a) Masking of personally identifiable information (PII)
  (b) Encryption during data transmission (TLS/HTTPS)
  (c) Access rights management and access control
